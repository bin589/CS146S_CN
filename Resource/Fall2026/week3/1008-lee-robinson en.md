## Bio

Default · Long

I’m an engineer and writer. I work on ML at SpaceX (formerly at [Cursor](https://leerob.com/cursor)), where I help train useful AI models. Previously, I worked at [Vercel](https://leerob.com/vercel) on Next.js. I’ve been coding for 15 years and teaching for the second half.

My life’s work is to make technology easy to understand and interesting to learn about. I’m a husband to my much cooler wife, a father to two radiant daughters, and a massive music fan. I also advise companies and [angel invest](mailto:lee@leerob.com).
