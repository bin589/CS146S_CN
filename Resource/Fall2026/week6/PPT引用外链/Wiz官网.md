Meet the agents
AI-Powered Attacker
Continuously simulate real attack paths across your cloud and AI stack to prove what's actually exploitable. By eliminating theoretical noise, you can instantly surface the high-priority risks that matter most in the AI era.
Autonomous Investigator
Autonomously investigate alerts across cloud and code to deliver defensible verdicts and orchestrate response. By correlating evidence end-to-end, you instantly expose the full blast radius and eliminate manual investigation overhead.
Precision Remediation
Bridge the gap between finding and fixing with context-aware remediation at the source. Automatically generate actionable plans, assign ownership, and execute fixes to drive your teams toward zero critical risk.

How Wiz helps: From Exposure to Code Fix
Attack surface scanning

- Attack surface scanner (reachable, exploitable)
- Effective internet-exposure of endpoints & services
- Wiz Threat Research intel
Deep internal analysis
We connect code, cloud, identities, network, and runtime to model:
- Lateral movement
- Privilege escalation
- Data access chains
FIX AT SCALE IN CODE
Using graph context and ownership mapping, Wiz:
- Identifies the right place for the fix
- Assigns the right owner (team, repo, service)
- Generates direct code & infra fixes to code owner
